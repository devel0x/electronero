How it works

  • Stake once per week – double stakes are disabled so choose your amount carefully.
  • Your staked IGP is reserved and removed from transfers until you unstake.
  • Payouts are handled the following week in ITC, as usual for the ambassador pool.
  • You may unstake manually before payouts, and admins can bulk unstake through the console.
